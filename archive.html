How ordering works

Close

  1. Open the order form. Clicking “Buy print” takes you to a Google Form where the artwork is pre-selected.
  2. Fill in your details. Add your name, email, and shipping address (and any notes).
  3. Payment after submission. Once the form is submitted, you’ll proceed to the next page to complete the payment. You’ll receive a separate confirmation once the print has been shipped.

Data collection and privacy (brief)

I collect only the information necessary to process your order, such as your name, email address, your delivery address and order details.

Payments are processed securely via Stripe. I do not receive or store your payment card details — they are handled entirely by Stripe. Order details submitted through Google Forms are stored in my Google account for the purpose of processing your order.

Order-related data is stored only for as long as necessary to fulfill the order and to meet accounting and legal obligations. You may request access to or deletion of your personal data by contacting me.

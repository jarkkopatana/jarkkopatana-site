Finnish light and moments of stillness

Hi! I'm Jarkko, a photographer from Finland. I try to bring my own perspective into each image.

I photograph Finnish light and moments of quiet attention.
My work is about slowing down — staying with an image long enough for it to begin speaking back.

Sometimes the subjects are landscapes, sometimes small details, sometimes abstractions formed by light itself. I’m interested in stillness, presence, and the space between seeing and feeling.

Data collection and privacy (brief)

I collect only the information necessary to process your order, such as your name, email address, your delivery address and order details. The information is used solely for order processing, communication, and delivery.

Payments are processed securely via Stripe. I do not receive or store your payment card details — they are handled entirely by Stripe. Order details submitted through Google Forms are stored in my Google account for the purpose of processing your order.

Order-related data is stored only for as long as necessary to fulfill the order and to meet accounting and legal obligations. You may request access to or deletion of your personal data by contacting me.
